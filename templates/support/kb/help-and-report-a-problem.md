# Help: ask a question or report a problem
platforms: both

**Help** at the bottom of the sidebar (above **Settings**) opens the Help panel with three tabs: **Ask**, **Report a problem** and **My reports**. Esc closes it; your chat and an unfinished report stay until you quit the app.

## Ask

1. Type your question ("Ask a question, or paste an error or screenshot") or click a suggestion. Enter sends, Shift + Enter starts a new line.
2. To show a picture: paste it (Ctrl + V on Windows, ⌘ + V on Mac), drop it on the panel, or click the camera button for a screenshot of the app (up to 3).
3. Under each answer, **Did this help?** The thumbs down opens **Report a problem**. If the answer is not in the help guide, a **Report this problem** button shows.

Ask needs Claude Code installed and signed in. It explains the app; it does not change your projects.

## Report a problem

Most red error messages have a **Report** button that opens this tab with the message filled in.

1. Chatted first? **Fill in from my chat** writes the report for you.
2. **Title** and **What happened?** are needed. **What did you expect?**, **Steps to make it happen** and **Error message** (paste it exactly) are optional.
3. **Screenshots**: **Capture the app**, **Add a picture** or paste (up to 3). They are public in the post: check they show nothing private.
4. **Add my app version and system** is ticked by default. **What will be posted** shows the post.
