# Model and effort (which Claude, how hard it thinks)
keywords: model, claude model, default model, ai model, opus, sonnet, haiku, opus 5.5, sonnet 5.5, haiku 5.5, account default, change model, effort, thinking, reasoning, think harder, low, medium, high, extra high, max, slow edits, faster, speed, usage, tokens, uses too much, quality, gpt model, chatgpt model
platforms: both

Both are in **Settings > AI engines**. They apply to every project that has no model or effort of its own.

## Model

- **Account default** (default): whatever your Claude Code account is set to.
- **Opus 5.5 (most capable)**
- **Sonnet 5.5 (faster, lighter on your plan)**
- **Haiku 5.5 (fastest, lightest on your plan)**

The list also has a **ChatGPT** group once ChatGPT (Codex) is installed and signed in, and an **Experimental (Google)** group once Settings > Experimental is on and signed in.

## Effort

How much Claude thinks before acting on edits and builds. Higher is slower and uses more of your plan.

- **Low (fastest)**
- **Medium (default)**
- **High**
- **Extra high**
- **Max (slowest)**

Good to know:
- Script research runs at medium unless a project has its own effort.
- On a ChatGPT model, Effort is Codex's reasoning effort.
- Google models have no effort setting: Effort then applies only to runs on Claude (Ultraedit, the Style Studio, and any run that falls back to Claude).

## One project only

A project can have its own model and effort: in New project (This project's settings), in Project settings in the editor, or in the menu under its Claude panel's input. Ultraedit uses them too; a project with none of its own runs Ultraedit on Opus 5.5 at High.

## Tips

- Edits feel slow or use too much of your plan: try Sonnet 5.5 or a lower Effort.
- Hard edits that keep failing: try Opus 5.5 or a higher Effort.
