## 2. Why the DISCIPLINE hook works

`[1] statement on black` → `[2] ritual everyone knows` → `[3] ritual fails + one-word red verdict` → `[4] antagonist + DAY counter`, all inside 15 s.
- "Every day…" is the 2nd beat of the Story Spine → the brain expects "but one day". Past tense = a story with an ending.
- "the same." names a pain without a judging label (no "procrastination" lecture).
- Readable in < 2 s muted. No logo, no title, no music swell before the first word.
- Self-recognition at 0:03, light humour (comic delay, "again.") lowers defences before the heavy middle.

## 3. Hook-line templates (4–8 words, past tense or "again", no question, no topic jargon, one period)

| ID | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Every [time] [started/ended] the same. | Every night ended the same. |
| H2 | He [did ritual] every [time]. Just in case. | He checked the door every night. Just in case. |
| H3 | [Number] [unit]. [Zero] [result]. | 312 messages read. Zero sent. |
| H4 | He always said "[excuse]." | He always said "tomorrow." |
| H5 | Nobody noticed [subtle sign]. | Nobody noticed he stopped calling. |
| H6 | It was [time]. Again. | It was 3:07 a.m. Again. |
| H7 | [Past statement]. Until [cut]. | He was fine. Until the house got quiet. |
| H8 | He used to [joyful thing]. (Archetype B) | He used to laugh a lot. |
| H9 | [Object] was always [state]. | The second chair was always empty. |
| H10 | The world used to be full of [keyword]. (B, tint keyword) | The world used to be full of people. |

Pairing rules: ritual ≤ 3 actions readable in silhouette; involves a **prop that returns at the bookend**;
verdict = 1 word + period, lowercase, red; failure must be *visible* (clock jump, cold mug, dust, stack).

## 5. Cut & text budget per act

| Act | Max shot | Avg shot | Text beats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 4 s | 3 s | hook, excuse, verdict |
| Act 1 loop | 8 s (shadow intro) | 3–7 s (one long held shot per DAY scene) | 1–2 red lines per scene |
| Mirror | 10 s | 5 s | identity + prophecy |
| Rock bottom | **10 s, the single longest still take** | — | 2 white couplets |
| Turn | 4 s | 2 s | DAY 1 |
| Grind | 6 s | 3–4 s, cuts on the beat | one callback per vignette + counters |
| Boss | 7 s | 4–5 s | couplet + boss line |
| Summit | 8 s | 6 s | couplet |
| End card | ≥ 5 s hold | — | title + slogan |

- Something must change every ~2 s *somewhere* (camera push counts, a particle, a prop digit, text). Bodies may hold.
- Text hold = max(1.2 s, 0.8 s + 0.3 s × words) (+0.5 s for lessons). Picture first, caption punchline after.
- One pattern interrupt every 8–15 s, rotating: location · scale · tempo · palette · typography event · sound event · number change. Never the same type twice in a row.

## 6. Open loops

At any time after 0:05 at least two questions are open and a new one opens before an old one closes:
"until when?" (hook) · "will he get up?" (alarm) · "who is that?" (shadow, closed by the mirror) · "how long?" (DAY counter, closed by reset) · "is he really lazy?" (closed by reframe) · "will he quit?" (grind) · "will it come back?" (boss) · "what happened to it?" (tiny shadow) · "what's the lesson?" (title).

Shareable spikes to design for: the verdict word · the reframe line · words shattering · passing through the giant · summit couplet · slogan.

## 7. Retention killers (reject the script if any is true)

First 15 s: logo/intro/subscribe ask · hook > 8 words or needs re-reading · names the topic abstractly ("Loneliness is…") · question hook · happy low-stakes opening without a text hook · niche ritual · mismatch with title/thumbnail · black held > 4 s.
Middle: low point after 45 % · > 10 s without new information · two montages with the same rhythm · insults never paid off · cartoon-evil voice (it must sound like the viewer's own reasonable excuse) · DAY counter moving position · turn without the reframe · instant cure · preaching (white lessons) during the fall · illegible text.
Ending: no bookend · slogan > 6–8 words or two slogans · end screen starting before the slogan · ending sad/ambiguous · runtime > 2:45 without reason.
