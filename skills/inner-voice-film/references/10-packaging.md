# 10 — Packaging: title, thumbnail, description, Shorts, release

Full analysis, 22-title library with alternates, sources: `deep-research/10-packaging-strategy.md`.

## 1. Why DISCIPLINE hit 3.1 M (≈221 K views/day vs ≤ 22 K for every other film)

It broke out on a channel with no audience, so the package did it: a **universal pain with a word people already
use about themselves** · "| An Animated Short Film" = a story, not a lecture · a visible enemy · 2:39 runtime ·
self-recognition in 3 s · bookend ending that invites rewatch · meme-ready slogans · a comment ritual ("What DAY
are you on?") · "made from scratch" credibility. Abstract words (ROOTS, READY, ONE LAST TIME) carried no known pain
(4.6–8.9 K/day); story titles ("How One Old Book Fixed His Life", 2.8 K/day) lost the film framing; 4-minute
films underperformed.

## 2. Titles

| Rank | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A (default) | `{PAIN WORD} \| An Animated Short Film` — ALL CAPS, no period, identical suffix every time | `OVERTHINKING \| An Animated Short Film` |
| B | `{Accusation}. {Reframe}.` | `You're Not Lonely. You're Hiding.` |
| C | `{Belief} Will {Betray} You.` | `Closure Will Never Come.` |
| D | `{Imperative} {pain} \| An Animated Short Film` | `Stop Comparing \| An Animated Short Film` |
| ✗ | abstract poetic words, "How X fixed his life" | — |

Pain word: 1–2 words, ≤ 12 chars ideally, searched/said about oneself, allows a 1–3 word shadow line.
Use B/C for ~1 in 3 uploads and as Test & Compare alternates.

## 3. Thumbnail

**Superseded:** use the `other-self-thumbnail` skill (measured from the real viral thumbnails: one face, one
effortful emotion, gaze to one lit object, one topic-hue light, 1–3 white caps words ending in a period, NO
shadow antagonist, blue only as a hint; SVG generator + optional Higgsfield finish). The notes below were written
before the thumbnails were analysed and are partly wrong (red lowercase text, Goliath template).

(old notes:) render as its own 1280×720 `<Still>`, never a raw screenshot

Near-black + one accent (brand blue) + Moon head (the sprout must read at thumbnail size). Hero ≥ 30 % height,
shadow twin ≥ 60 %. Text 0–3 words = **the shadow's line, accent-coloured lowercase handwritten** (never the title word) or no text. Nothing important bottom-right (duration badge)
or bottom 10 %. Drop letterbox and DAY counter. Shadow glow ×1.5, soft spotlight on the hero.
Templates: **T1 Goliath** (giant shadow right, small determined hero lower-left — default for fight topics) ·
**T2 Mirror** (reflection = shadow; self-image topics) · **T3 Spotlight** (sad topics) · **T4 Topic object**
(oversized red phone/clock/heart). A/B: T1 with text · T1 no text · alternate template; judge by watch-time share.

## 4. Description template

```
{Hook line}. {Fragment}. {Fragment}. {The voice's lie}.

{TITLE} is a short animated film about {the war against / the weight of / the voice behind} {pain}, and the moment you {decision}.

{Lesson line A.} {Lesson line B.}

0:00 {Hook sentence}
0:40 The mirror
0:50 Rock bottom
1:00 Day 1
1:12 The grind
1:49 The final fight
2:04 The summit
2:25 {Slogan}

Every frame, sound and note of music in this film was made from scratch.

Subscribe to The Other Self for more short films about the person we are, and the one we could become.

#{word} #shortfilm #animation
```
Chapters: one per line, first `0:00`, ≥ 3, each ≥ 10 s (derive real timestamps from `sceneStarts()`).
Keep the credibility line only if literally true (AI/licensed audio → "Every frame of this film was animated from scratch").
Replace the channel name with the user's channel. Max 3 hashtags.

## 5. Pinned comment (rotate)

Ritual: "What DAY are you on? Comment it and come back in 30 days." · Confession: "What does YOUR shadow say to
you? Write what you'll say back." · Vote: "Next film: X or Y? Most-liked reply decides."

## 6. Shorts (separate 1080×1920 compositions, reflowed, 15–35 s, loopable)

Mirror ("the enemy was never outside.") · punching words · "you can't." giant · bookend contrast ("same alarm.
different man.") · "told you." push-up. Post 2–4 within 3 days of the film; link via related video + pinned comment.

## 7. Hook test plan (three openings)

YouTube can't A/B-test a video's opening, so test hooks through Shorts: render the same 20–30 s opening with
3 different hook lines / first shots as three Shorts (same everything else), post them 1–2 days apart, compare
"viewed vs swiped" % and average view duration. Use the winner's pattern for the long film (or re-cut the next
film). Log hook template ID → result to learn which hook types work for this audience.

## 8. Release & measurement

2–3 films/week while a video does > 20 K/day, otherwise 1–2 with higher craft; never sacrifice hook, antagonist
or 2:30–3:30 length for volume (YouTube's "inauthentic/repetitive" policy). Suggested order after DISCIPLINE/
DOPAMINE: PROCRASTINATION → OVERTHINKING → COMFORT ZONE → SCROLLING → LONELINESS → COMPARISON → FEAR → SELF-WORTH
→ ANXIETY → HEARTBREAK (grief/love/anger later). Measure: CTR vs channel median, ≥ 70 % still watching at 0:30,
≥ 60 % average viewed, traffic mix, subs per 1 K views, "DAY X" comments. 7-day post-mortem per upload.
