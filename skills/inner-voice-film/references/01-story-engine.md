## 1. The formula

A silent, unnamed hero (our Moon head) repeats a **daily micro-ritual**. A **voice** — first only accent-coloured
text, then his own black twin with a glowing rim — talks him out of the hard choice in short lowercase lines.
**DAY counters** jump while he decays. The voice becomes **his own reflection**. At **rock bottom** under a
spotlight the white narrator **reframes his identity** ("X isn't who you are. It's what you repeat.") and
switches from "He" to "you". The **same ritual shot** returns and this time he makes the opposite choice:
a new **DAY 1** and a visible **transformation token** (DISCIPLINE: red headband). In the **grind**, every old
red line is **physically defeated**. The voice returns as a **giant final boss** and is split. At the
**summit** (the only warm palette) a white couplet lands. A **bookend** repeats the ritual shot with the voice
now **tiny and powerless**. End on **one title word** + a **chiasmus slogan**.

Story Spine: *Every day* he … → *But one day* he believed the voice → *Because of that* he hit bottom →
*Until finally* the same moment came and he chose differently → *Ever since then* the voice is tiny.

## 1b. The logic ("the brain") — copy THIS, not the surface

Copy the structure, never the surface (characters' lines, scenes, music, slogans). Every section has exactly
**one job**; a scene that both shows the problem and offers the cure is broken.

| Function (one job) | What the viewer feels | DISCIPLINE's answer | Your film must supply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook: problem + conflict | "that's me" | snooze, "again." | a universal ritual that fails |
| Repetition + voice introduced | "I know that voice" | couch, red shadow | the excuse personified |
| Progress counter + exhaustion | "time is being wasted" | DAY 25/47, mirror | a counter in the topic's own unit |
| Rock bottom | empathy, quiet | crying under a spotlight | the lowest, stillest image |
| Turn + symbol | hope, decision | alarm turned off calmly, headband | one imitable gesture + one token |
| Montage, rising sound, one-line slogans | momentum, inspiration | run, push-ups, summit | effort shown, voice lines defeated |
| Loop closure + call | satisfaction, rewatch | same room, "Day one" | same place/time as the opening + a choice |

Why the pieces exist:
- **Ritual + symbol** give the viewer something to *imitate* and an identity to adopt (people comment "DAY 1",
  wear the headband idea). Pick a gesture a viewer could do tomorrow morning.
- **Counter** makes invisible progress visible and works as a progress bar for the film itself. Choose the unit
  from the topic: days, messages sent, pages written, km run, money saved, lines of code, hours offline.
- **Sound map** carries the emotion (no narrator VO; only the twin's lines are spoken with ElevenLabs — 08-audio §0): plan music density, silence and SFX per section (08-audio).
- **Quotable lines**: 1–2 screenshot-able lines in the final third + the slogan.
- **Loop closure** sends the viewer back to the start (rewatch).
- **Negativity dose**: the voice may be harsh only until rock bottom (≈35–40 % of runtime). After the turn the
  tone is hope/determination; the voice loses every exchange. Never end on cruelty, never more than ~40 % of the
  film in decline, never shame the viewer — the reframe always restores agency.

## 2. Two archetypes

| | **A · Loop of Failure** (DISCIPLINE, 3.1 M) — DEFAULT | **B · Paradise Lost** (DOPAMINE, 283 K) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook card | present-feeling routine: "Every day started the same." | past tense loss: "The world used to be full of color." (keyword tinted) |
| First scene | the failure ritual (viewer recognises self by 0:03) | the ideal state (colourful hobbies, friend) — no problem yet |
| Antagonist entry | 0:13 (≈8 %) | ≈0:25 after a "resist once, then relapse" beat |
| Decay meter | DAY counter jumps | colour drains + topic HUD (dopamine graph) |
| Low point | 0:50 (≈32 %) | ≈1:20 (≈50 %) — too late |

**Use A by default.** Use B only for topics defined by *losing* something (grief, heartbreak, addiction,
burnout) — and even then: put the self-recognition/failure stamp before 0:15 and keep rock bottom ≤ 40 %.
Borrowed B devices that are always allowed: keyword-tinted hook, topic HUD, colour as the stake, the
"resist once, then relapse" beat, reward particles.

Act proportions: Fall (B00–B06) 37.5 % · Rise (B07–B11) 47.5 % · Close 15 %.
Hard windows: failure stamp < 0:15 · rock bottom 31–40 % · turn 36–40 % · boss 72–80 % · warm palette ≈78 %.
Longer films (3:30–4:30): add a decay vignette, 1–2 grind activities, optionally a relapse mini-beat inside
B08 (misses a day, voice says "see?", counter continues). Never lengthen B00–B03 (keep < 25 s).

## 4. The 12 rules

1. **Bookend ×3**: the ritual shot appears in B01 (fail), B07 (choose), B12 (own it) with identical framing. Only action, light, token and shadow-size change.
2. **Voice ledger**: every red line in Act 1 is paid off by a physical action in Act 2, in the same shot as the line. The hero never answers verbally.
3. **Antagonist arc**: text only → whisperer behind furniture (1.1×) → companion touching his chair (1.2×) → his reflection (1.0×) → absent (he believes it) → disembodied floating words → giant (2.5–3×) → tiny (0.5×). Act 1 escalates by closeness, Act 2 by size. Size grows with *desperation*, not power.
4. **Voice-line grammar**: lowercase, 1–4 words, ends with a period, never "!". Ladder: rationalisation → imperative → fake empathy → futility → identity → prophecy → "told you." → label list → absolute negation. Boss line = shortest line in the film. (Exception: OVERTHINKING speaks in "what if…?" questions.)
5. **Lesson couplets** (white): two cards, line 2 reuses and flips a word/structure of line 1 (save → save yourself; gets you → keeps you).
6. **Pronoun shift**: narration is 3rd person past tense until the reframe line, then 2nd person present ("you") for the rest of the film.
7. **Title word** = theme = end card, ALL CAPS one abstract noun naming a pain people already say about themselves. It appears on screen only in couplet 1 and the end card.
8. **Chiasmus slogan** ≤ 6 words, a binary choice, T-shirt-printable ("Day one, or one day."). Optional "You decide."
9. **Silent, unnamed, universal hero**. No dialogue, no brands, no country markers. All text is narrator (white), voice/info (accent colour), counters (gray), reps (glowing white).
10. **DAY counters** on every scene B03–B10 only. Decay: careless big jumps (1 → 47 → 88 → 365). Grind: restart at DAY 1, then roughly geometric (3, 12, 64, 150, 190, 270). Not on hook, rock bottom, summit, bookend, card.
11. **Palette as plot**: mono + accent for the whole film; blue = seduction (screens), amber = effort (lamps), warm dawn **only** at the summit and bookend. Rock bottom has no colour at all. In Act 3 the accent colour becomes the hero's (token, underline).
12. **Effort is visible**: sweat, gritted teeth, shaking, a stop-and-restart. Never effortless success. Never depict self-harm; the reframe always restores agency.

## 5. Topic Adaptation Engine (input: TITLE + TOPIC → filled beat sheet)

| Step | Produce | Test |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Core struggle | "He keeps choosing **[easy]** instead of **[hard]** because a voice says **[lie]**." | Would a 14- and a 45-year-old both say "that's me"? |
| 2 Micro-ritual | location + object showing a number/time + moment of the day + easy choice | drawable in < 10 SVG shapes; shows a number; can bookend |
| 3 Antagonist identity | "the voice of ___" + what it pretends to protect him from | kind at first, cruel later |
| 4 Excuse + verdict | red typed excuse ≤ 5 words with "..." ("just / one more / later") + one-word verdict ("again." "still." "alone.") | verdict works as one word |
| 5 Voice ledger (6–10 lines) | lines in ladder order, each paired with its grind payoff | every line has a payoff |
| 6 Decay vignettes (3–5) | avoidance spreading into different life areas | each a different room/area |
| 7 Mirror line | "you're [label]. you'll never [change]." | label is a real self-belief |
| 8 Rock bottom + reframe | "He [verb]-ed it." / "Day after day." · "But [label] isn't who you are." / "It's what you [behaviour verb]." | B2 ends on a verb the grind reverses |
| 9 Bookend change + token | instant opposite action at B07 + one single-colour token (see 05 §4) | token readable at 300 px hero height |
| 10 Grind (4–6) | G1 struggle-continue · G2 "told you." + rep counter · G3 destroy-the-words verb (punch/tear/erase/wash/blow away; grief: dissolve into light) · G4 proof + mirror reversal | each mirrors a decay scene |
| 11 Couplet 1 + boss | "[fake fix] gets you ___." / "[TITLE] keeps you ___." · giant blocks a threshold with ≤ 3 words | hero never stops moving |
| 12 Summit couplet + slogan | hard truth / empowerment reusing a word · chiasmus ≤ 6 words | slogan reverses meaningfully |

## 6. Screenplay output format (write scripts exactly like this)

Header:
```
TITLE: LONELINESS
YT_TITLE: LONELINESS | An Animated Short Film
ARCHETYPE: A (Loop of Failure)
LOGLINE: <one sentence>
RUNTIME: 160s @ 30fps = 4800 frames | 1920x1080 | letterbox 96/96
HOOK: "Every night ended the same."
RITUAL: <location> | <object+number> | <time> | easy = <…> | new = <…>
TOKEN: <colour + object> (appears B07)
ACCENT: <hex> (shadow glow, voice text, HUD hits)
HUD: none | <label + behaviour>
VOICE_LEDGER:
  V1 "<line>" -> paid <beat> (<action>)
  ...
COUPLET_1 / COUPLET_2 / SLOGAN
PALETTE_ARC / MUSIC (M1 piano … M5 outro, BPM)
```
One block per shot:
```
### S07 — B04 DECAY (2/4)
FRAMES:     660–870 (22.0–29.0s, 210f)
SETTING:    desk | wall clock spinning | lamp pool ON | floor y=756
COUNTER:    DAY roll 1→47 at 3–42 (ease-out, hard digit swaps)
CHARACTERS: HERO pose=deskSlump expr=exhausted (held) · SHADOW rise-from-floor 3–36, hand on chair, glow 1.0
ACTION:     0–20 lamp ramps up · 3–36 shadow rises · then HOLD (one mover at a time)
TEXT:       [VOICE] "you're tired." 45–123 on head, drift up · [VOICE] "why even try?" 135–219 ×1.2, over eyes, glow ×1.5
SFX:        rumble 30 · whisper layer under each red line · clock tick
MUSIC:      M1 continues
CAMERA:     push-in 1.00→1.075 linear, origin (48%,52%)
TRANSITION: dip-to-black out 10f / in 20f
NOTES:      callback for G1 (run)
```
Footer check line: `CHECK: hook<=4s | stamp<0:15 | DAY1 x2 | bottom@31–40% | boss@72–80% | ledger paid | title word only B09+B13 | bookend framing B01=B07=B12 | hero lines 0 | pronoun switch B06 | warm only B11–B12`

## 7. QA before any code

- [ ] Each shot has one job (state it in NOTES) · counter unit fits the topic · decline ≤ 40 % of runtime
- [ ] The turn gesture is something a viewer could imitate · 1–2 quotable lines in the last third
- [ ] No line, scene or slogan copied from a reference film

- [ ] Hook passes 02-hook constraints · failure stamp before 0:15
- [ ] Every red line paired with a payoff · boss line is the shortest
- [ ] Rock bottom at 31–40 % · turn ≤ 40 % · boss 72–80 %
- [ ] B01/B07/B12 framing identical · token appears at B07 and never leaves
- [ ] One message at a time · white and red never in the same frame
- [ ] ≤ 16 text cards total · every narrator line ≤ 7 words, voice ≤ 4
- [ ] Runtime 2:30–2:45 (unless the user asks otherwise)
