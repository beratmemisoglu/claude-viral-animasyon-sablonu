Source: screenshot of the whole "Day One Films" video grid → crops in the project

the newer ones (IS THIS IT? · WAKE UP. · WHAT IF? · DISCIPLINE. · ONE LAST TIME. · test paper "F" ·

our clean vector / soft 3D renders as "çizimde kötü" (weak drawing). This file overrides earlier style notes.

## What their drawing does (copy the craft, not the character)

| Element | Their thumbnails | Our earlier renders (too weak) |
|---|---|---|
| **Eyes** | big round eyeballs with WHITE sclera, small dark pupils, **bloodshot pink veins**, **dark sunken eye bags/rings**, red rims; the eyes carry the whole emotion | solid black ovals — cute, low emotion |
| **Hands** | big, detailed five-finger cartoon hands with knuckle lines, gripping (blanket, barbell, paper) or reaching toward the camera with foreshortening | round mitten blobs |
| **Face detail** | forehead wrinkles, many sweat beads, gritted teeth / open mouth with tongue, redness under the eyes | almost none |
| **Line** | thick, variable-width black ink lines, hand-inked feel; textured, scratchy painterly shading in shadows | thin uniform vector line |
| **Light** | one warm golden key light → glowing rim along the head edge; the emitter (pills, F, phone) is the only saturated colour; everything else dark, desaturated, grainy | flat or dim brownish light |
| **Emotion** | always high negative arousal: dread, desperation, exhaustion, strain, defeat — never "oops/cute" | worried-cute |
| **Composition** | dynamic diagonals, foreshortening (hand reaching at camera), extreme close-ups, big iconic props (barbell plates, spilled glowing pills, crumpled test with red F, armchair in a spotlight, black smoke tendrils) | static, frontal, small props |
| **Text** | very chunky rounded bubbly white letters with a thin dark outline + soft shadow, slightly hand-made/uneven, 1–3 words; **question marks ARE used** ("IS THIS IT?", "WHAT IF?"); text sometimes equals the title (ONE LAST TIME.) | Fredoka 700 — close enough; keep but allow "?" |

## Rules for our thumbnails (Moon head stays ours)

1. Keep identity: big perfectly round bald sand-coloured head, the leaf sprout, our blue shadow twin (same
   round head + sprout). Body may become more expressive (detailed hands, posture) in thumbnails.
2. **Thumbnail eye LOD**: in thumbnails the eyes are big round eyeballs with white sclera + small pupils (+ veins and
   eye bags when tired/afraid). The films keep the simple oval eyes. (Decision 2026-10-14 after the grid review;
   confirm with the user if in doubt.)
3. Always: detailed hands doing something, sweat/wrinkles as the emotion needs, one warm rim light, gritty inked
   painterly texture, high-arousal negative emotion, one saturated emitter.
4. Never feed competitor thumbnails to the AI as references; describe the craft in words (prompt below).
