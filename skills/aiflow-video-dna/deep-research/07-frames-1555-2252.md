### A3. How the CapCut timeline is shown

- Always the full CapCut window at 1:1. The viewer reads values off the property panels; nothing is magnified.
- Order of operations on screen: import voice → trim the silent tail → import AI clips and **mute the video track** → import stills → **drag right edges to the narration** → whole-timeline overview (17:58) → Animation tab → **Scale keyframe 100 %→110 %** (18:05–18:09) → Auto captions → caption template + effect → adjustment layer (vignette) + filter → Export.
- Each step is a 3–20 s burst followed by a jump cut. Long manual work (placing ~35 visuals) is a montage of jump cuts **under continuous narration**, so the narration is never paused.
- The most-replayed moment in the range is **18:04–18:18 (heatmap 0.53)**: the keyframe zoom and animation menu. The minimum (0.03) is the silent placing montage, 17:09–17:50. Viewers rewind exact settings and skip repetitive manual work.

### B2. Pacing: narration vs image changes

- English TTS narration: **165 wpm including pauses, ~183 wpm excluding them**. Sentence pauses are 0.32–0.62 s; pauses at
  cuts measure −45 to −55 LUFS (true silence).
- One image per **1–2 sentences** (≈15–30 words). Every image change falls on a clause start (e.g. T+10.65 on the
  "because" clause; T+51.2 on the "not because" clause). An image never changes mid-word.
- The tutorial states the rule out loud (17:05–17:50): image length is free; change the image when the concept changes.
  One sentence can get one image, or three sentences can share one.
- Stated style rule (18:27–18:36): **slow-paced editing** that a child or an elderly viewer can follow; avoid fast-paced editing.

## Why it works

1. **The tutorial edit trades polish for proof of reality.** It is a full-screen, unzoomed recording of real tools and real values
   (autosave clock, file names, sizes, progress bars), compressed ~10:1 with jump cuts. Narration is continuous at ~209 wpm with breaths
   removed. Viewers can copy settings straight off the frame: the 18:04–18:18 keyframe/animation segment is the most-replayed spot
   in this range (0.53), while the silent manual-placing montage is the least (0.03).
2. **Proof inserts re-motivate before the "boring" admin steps.** The 8.7-s finished preview at 19:28 and two rounds of 950 K-view channel
   pages sit just before the thumbnail and SEO sections, and those sections hold a flat ~0.13–0.24 replay level instead of collapsing.
3. **The lead magnet drives engagement.** A static 4.2-s "200 Likes" card plus a PDF promise yields a 3.1 % like rate and
   133 comments, 26 of them about the PDF.
4. **The taught style is cheap, readable and broad.** One mascot on white; one image per ~1–2 sentences (mean 7.3 s in the excerpt,
   ~11–12 s across the timeline); a corner-only vignette that keeps the centre clean; big word-by-word captions in the lower
   band for sound-off viewing; deliberately slow pacing for older, high-RPM finance audiences. Only a few AI-animated clips are needed
   (front-loaded in the first 22 s) and a few 0.5-s In-animations hide that most of the video is stills.
5. **Weak spot:** the ending. The example cuts off mid-video with no outro, end screen or music; replay falls to 0.00. Some comments
   also ask for better editing and question the voice.

## Rules to copy

### (A) Tutorial-edit rules (the meta edit)

1. Record every tool **full-screen at 100 %**. Add no digital zooms on UI. Let the app's own zoom/scroll and a **red click ring** on every click carry emphasis.
2. Show each workflow step as a **3–20 s burst**, then jump-cut. Keep a time compression of **≈10:1** for manual work and up to **15:1** for repetitive placement; never show more than 20 s of repetitive dragging.
3. Keep narration **continuous over all jump cuts**. Cut breaths so 400 ms loudness rarely falls below **−35 LUFS** in the tutorial voice.
4. Hold the screen **≥10 s on any exact numeric setting** the viewer must copy (keyframe values, durations, export settings). Narrate the value while it is visible.
5. Show settings in the order **voice → trim silence → AI clips (mute) → stills → timing → animation/keyframes → auto captions → caption style → adjustment layer/filter → export dialog**.
6. Insert a **full-screen preview of the finished result (6–10 s)** right after the edit and before the thumbnail/SEO steps.
7. Use **2–3 social-proof B-roll segments of 8–20 s** (channel pages sorted by Popular, view counts visible) under motivational narration.
8. Show the thumbnail step end-to-end in **≤75 s**: prompt doc → LLM (append title) → image generator (no text) → one follow-up asking for **1–2 words of headline text on top, character left, elements right** → final variants.
9. Show SEO in **≤30 s**: one prompt run, then scroll through 5 titles / description / tags / keywords / hashtags while highlighting each block.
10. CTA block in the last 40 s before the example:
    - verbal subscribe ask over the channel page (8–10 s);
    - one **static full-screen text card** for the like target: lime #3CFD00 heavy rounded sans, text height **≈14 % of H**, width **≈50 % of W**, background darkened to **≈10–15 %**, **4 ± 0.5 s**, hard cut in and out;
    - the lead-magnet promise.
11. Enter the finished example with a **hard cut** after one goodbye line. To improve on the source, add a 3–5 s branded outro and end screen after the example instead of ending mid-video (its replay fell to 0.00).
12. Use **hard cuts only** in the tutorial layer. No dissolves, no SFX, no music bed under the instructions.

### (B) Finance-explainer rules (the style being taught)

1. **Canvas:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 mp4, Rec.709 SDR, "Recommended" bitrate (~402 MB for 6:44).
2. **Visual cadence:**
   - one visual per **1–2 sentences (15–30 words)**;
   - shot length **3–11 s in the first minute (mean ≈7 s)** and **≈11–12 s mean over the whole video**;
   - never hold one visual longer than **20 s** (the source sometimes holds 28–32 s; avoid that);
   - cut only at clause or sentence starts.
3. **Front-load motion:** make the first **20–25 s** out of **3–4 AI image-to-video clips (≈6 s source each)** of the mascot gesturing. Reuse a talking-gesture clip at most once.
4. **Stills motion:** on ≈1 of every 3–4 stills, apply **scale 100 %→110 % over the still's full duration (≈0.9–1.1 %/s), centred, no pan, linear**. Leave the others static but give them a **0.5–0.7 s In-animation** (ink/curve wipe, swoosh slide with motion blur, or horizontal glitch smear).
5. **Transitions:** hard cut between AI clips. Use no cross-dissolves and no fades to black, and add no transition SFX.
6. **Background:** flat off-white **#FAFAF7** baked into every image. Add **≥40 % empty space** per image and keep the bottom **15 %** free of key content (caption zone).
7. **Vignette:** elliptical, corners only. Target corner **#595959**, 3 % diagonal **#797979**, 6 % **#A3A3A0**, 10 % **#D3D3D0**, untouched beyond 15 % of the diagonal; edge midpoints stay white. Apply as one adjustment layer across the whole timeline, optionally with a mild warm filter.
8. **Captions, content:** auto-generated, **lowercase, no punctuation**, **5–12 words (mean ≈7)** per caption, a new caption every **≈2.7 s**, 1–2 lines wrapping at **≈66 % of frame width**. **Proof-read numbers** (the source left price errors in).
9. **Captions, look:**
   - white **#F9F9F7** rounded geometric sans, semibold, no stroke;
   - line box height **≈6.5 % of H** (ascender ≈4.2 % of H);
   - a **black ragged brush-stroke box (#1E1E1E–#222, 85–90 % opacity)** behind each line;
   - phrase centred horizontally, block centred at **≈87 % of H**.
10. **Captions, animation:** word-by-word reveal synced to the word timestamps. The next word appears at ~40 % opacity, blurred, **≈2 % of H below the baseline**, then rises and turns white in **0.2–0.3 s**. Clear the caption **0.1–0.4 s** before the next phrase.
11. **Narration:** calm AI voice at **≈165 wpm with pauses (≈180 wpm without)**, sentence pauses **0.3–0.6 s**, mean ≈ −22 LUFS momentary. No music bed or SFX in the source. If you add music, keep it ≥ 20 dB under the voice and use no SFX on cuts.
12. **Mascot — ORIGINAL only.** Create a proprietary everyman:
    - big round head (≈35–40 % of visible body height), simple dot eyes, one-line nose, expressive wide mouth;
    - **thick uniform black outline ≈1 % of frame height (8–12 px at 1080p)**, flat colours with one cel-shade step, no gradients;
    - a distinctive signature item and palette of your own (e.g. a coloured hoodie or glasses);
    - do **not** reproduce the near-white-skin / swept-grey-hair / plain-blue-tee combination or any trait of a known TV cartoon character;
    - keep the mascot in **100 % of visuals**.
13. **Props and labels:** flat icon props in the same outline weight (coins, $ badges, phones, cars, houses, coffee, piggy bank, up/down arrows, simple charts). Use **at most 3 one-to-two-word labels per image**. Use green for gain and red for loss.
14. **Image prompts:** one prompt per scene, ~1 scene per 13 s of script. Always end with the same style suffix: "simple 2D cartoon finance explainer illustration, flat vector, bold outlines, clean white background".
15. **Thumbnail:** same generator and style; mascot left (≈40–45 % of width) with a worried or surprised face; right side shows a 3–5-icon chain with arrows; **two-word heavy black all-caps headline at the top**; white background.
16. **Ending (improve on the source):** close on a full sentence plus a **≥0.5 s** hold, then a 5–20 s end-screen plate. Never stop dead mid-script.

## Open questions / low-confidence

- **Vignette numeric value:** the field read "3" while being dragged (18:51). The slider was near the middle and the visual result is strong. The final value is unknown; use the measured luminance targets instead.
- **Exact In-animation names:** the three transition looks match In/Combo presets seen in the panel (an ink/curve wipe, a swoosh, "Smear…"-type glitch), but the clicks applying them to these clips were not shown. Mapping preset name → clip is inferred.
- **"Renoir" filter:** added off-camera between 18:48 and 18:49. Its strength and its contribution to the warm #FAFAF7 white are not verified.
- **Caption template name** was not legible. The style was measured from output frames.
- **Font family** is inferred visually (Poppins/Montserrat-like); CapCut showed "System" before the template was applied.
- The **glitch-looking frames at 20:47.4 and 20:55** in the tutorial are most likely screen-recorder artifacts while scrolling, not designed transitions.
- **Ease curve of the 100→110 % zoom:** measurements show near-zero change in the first ~1.4 s, then ≈linear. This could be a slight ease-in or keyframe placement a few frames after the cut.
- The full 6:44 export was not available; whole-timeline statistics (≈34–36 clips, durations 10–32 s) come from timeline labels and overview frames, not from the rendered file.
- The music/SFX absence is inferred from loudness minima. A very quiet bed under −45 LUFS cannot be fully excluded.
