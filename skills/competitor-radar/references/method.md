# Method — from competitor data to an original film

## 1. Diagnose (per top performer)
| Field | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Pain word | Which one-word struggle does the viewer feel? | "discipline", "overthinking", "loneliness" |
| Promise | What transformation/insight does the title imply? | "from snoozing to showing up" |
| Format signal | Length bucket, title form (ONE WORD \| …, "If …", question), Short or long | 2:40, ONE WORD |
| Hook type | absurd · inner voice visible · surreal metaphor · extreme emotion | inner voice visible |
| Why it travels | identity ("that's me") · universality · curiosity gap · timing (season, exams, New Year, Monday) | identity + Monday |
| Signal strength | 24h gain, outlier ×, views/day. Trend = ≥ 2 videos or ≥ 2 channels on the same pain | 2 channels, outlier 4.1× |

Ignore: raw lifetime views of very old videos, channels whose median is tiny (one fluke), anything off-niche.
Look for **gaps**: pains with demand on 2+ channels but no strong version yet, or a pain everyone treats the same way
(we bring a new metaphor).

## 2. Idea card (write ≥ 6)
```
TITLE: <ONE WORD, ALL CAPS, a pain people say about themselves>      e.g. OVERTHINKING
YT TITLE: <TITLE> | An Animated Short Film   (+ 1 alt title, ≤ 5 words, curiosity)
HOOK (B00): one past-tense sentence, 4–8 words
RITUAL: place + object that shows a number + the easy choice
TWIN'S LIE: the excuse ("just … ") + 3 escalating lines (lowercase, ≤ 4 words)
METAPHOR: one physical object that grows/shrinks with the struggle (must be NEW for the channel)
TOKEN: one colour token the hero earns at the turn (never red)
EVIDENCE: competitor data (channel · title · views/day or outlier ×) that proves demand
ORIGINALITY: what we do that nobody in the snapshot does
SHORTS: the 10-second moment that works vertical
```

## 3. Originality gate (reject if any is true)
- Title word already used by us (`PUBLISHED.md`, `TOPICS.md` [x]).
- Same title word **and** same angle as a competitor video from the last 60 days.
- Metaphor/set already used by us (seed/plant, clock-to-the-wall, laundry pile, …) or a recognisable competitor set-piece.
- Any line, character, plot beat or thumbnail composition that copies a competitor.
- The idea needs a second main character, dialogue, gore, self-harm, or real brands.

## 4. Scoring rubric (0–5 each, max 30)
| Criterion | 5 means |
|---|---|
| Demand | pain appears in ≥ 3 competitor top performers / high search intent word |
| Velocity | rising now (24h gain or ≤ 14-day views/day leaders) |
| Originality | nobody shows it with our angle/metaphor; passes the gate easily |
| Metaphor | one drawable object, grows in decay, reverses in the grind, readable silently |
| Universality | a 14- and a 45-year-old both say "that's me", no culture lock-in |
| Production fit | buildable with the existing studio library (sets, props) in one day |
Pick the highest total; tie-break: velocity, then originality. Never pick the same pain two days in a row.

## 5. BRIEF.md (input for inner-voice-film)
Write `project/films/<slug>/BRIEF.md` with the winning card + 3 hook variants + 2 alt titles + the evidence table rows.
`inner-voice-film` then writes SCREENPLAY.md from it (13 beats, voice ledger, couplets, slogan).

## 6. Examples of good transformations (logic, not surface)
- Signal: "If you … for 30 days" hypotheticals trending → our form stays ONE WORD, but the HOOK borrows the curiosity
  ("He gave it thirty days. The voice gave him one.").
- Signal: loneliness outliers on 2 channels, all set at night with a phone → our angle: the second chair at the table
  that slowly fills with unopened letters (new metaphor), not a phone.
